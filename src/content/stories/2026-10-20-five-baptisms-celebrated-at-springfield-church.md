The Springfield Seventh-day Adventist Church celebrated a high Sabbath on October 18 as five people committed their lives to God through baptism and profession of faith.

Photos shared by the church show the baptisms and the celebration that followed as the congregation welcomed the new members. Church member Danis Legra, who checked in from the service, described the day as a celebration of five people deciding to give their lives to God.

The baptisms capped a full weekend of worship and fellowship at the Springfield church, which also included a fellowship lunch and special music.
