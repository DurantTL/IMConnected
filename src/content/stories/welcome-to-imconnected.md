IMConnected is a story magazine for the Iowa-Missouri Conference of Seventh-day Adventists. It gathers news, features and testimonies from across both states in one place, so members can see how God is working in congregations beyond their own.

## Share what God is doing

Anyone can send in a story using the [Submit a Story](/submit/) page. Tell us who was involved, what happened, when and where it took place, and why it mattered to your church or community. Photos are welcome.

Every submission is reviewed by the communication department before it is published. Editors may shorten or adjust stories for clarity and style.

## What to read here

- **News** from the conference office, local churches, schools and camp
- **Features** with longer stories and personal testimonies
- **Columns** from conference leaders and ministry directors
- **Youth & Pathfinders** coverage of youth ministry, Pathfinders and Adventurers
- **Kids** content for younger readers
