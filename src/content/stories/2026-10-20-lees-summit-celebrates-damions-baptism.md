Sabbath, October 17 was a day of celebration at the Lee's Summit Seventh-day Adventist Church as Damion was baptized.

Photos shared by the church show Damion in the baptismal pool with his pastor, and a prayer of dedication at the pulpit following the baptism. The church also shared a video of the baptism, welcoming Damion with the words, "Happy Day! Welcome Damion!"

Baptisms are always a highlight in the life of a congregation, and Lee's Summit marked the occasion with congratulations and praise.
